## Description

Plan your UT Austin semester faster — without giving your data to anyone.

Fastlane Course Planner is an independent, student-built extension based on the open-source UT Registration Plus. It keeps everything UT Registration Plus does and adds:

• Grade percentages right on the course schedule: see "72.4% A-+" beside each section, calculated from real historical grade counts, with sample size, semesters, professor vs. course-wide scope, and source on hover. Choose your threshold (A, A-, B+ …).
• Asynchronous classes highlighted in blue — only when the listing shows an online instruction mode with no scheduled meetings. Live online classes get their own label.
• Conflicts flagged automatically: sections that overlap your schedule (including labs and discussions) are struck through and name the class they collide with. Missing times show "Conflict ?" instead of a guess.
• Ranked backup classes for every planned class that never clutter your calendar until you activate one.
• Degree audit import (HTML or PDF), parsed entirely in your browser, with a review screen for anything uncertain. Pending transfer credit is never counted as awarded.
• Degree-aware recommendations when your first choices are full, ranked by requirement, conflicts, your online/async and no-Friday preferences, and historical grades — with exact unique numbers and honest availability ("Check availability" unless UT just showed it open).
• A manual registration assistant on UT's add/drop page: next unique number, one-click copy, fallbacks, attempt history, undo. Optional autofill types the code for you; you always press Submit yourself.
• Registration reminders (24 h, 1 h, 10 min, custom), a campus map of your day, and a phone wallpaper of your schedule.

Private by design: no account, no server, no analytics. Your schedules, audit and registration plans stay in your browser. Delete everything with one click.

Fastlane never registers for you, never clicks UT's buttons, never refreshes pages or polls seats. UT prohibits automated registration programs.

## Permission justifications

| Permission | Why it is needed |
| --- | --- |
| `storage` | Save schedules, backups, the registration queue, reminders and settings in the browser. |
| `unlimitedStorage` | The bundled historical grade database (~13 MB), cached course sections and an imported degree audit can exceed the default quota. |
| `alarms` | Fire registration reminders at the times the student chooses, and check for dataset updates at most once a day. |
| `notifications` | Show those reminders. |
| `scripting` | Inherited from UT Registration Plus: fills the student's own course-evaluation lookup page (bluera.com) when the student opens it from the extension. |
| Host: `utdirect.utexas.edu/apps/registrar/course_schedule/*`, `registrar.utexas.edu/schedules/*`, `catalog.utexas.edu/ribbit/` | Show grades, delivery mode, conflicts and backup buttons on UT's public course listings. |
| Host: `utdirect.utexas.edu/registration/*` | Show the manual registration assistant on UT's add/drop screen. |
| Host: `utexas.collegescheduler.com/*`, `my.utexas.edu/student/*` | Inherited UT Registration Plus integrations (importing classes into the planner). |
| Host: `*.bluera.com/rpvlf.aspx*` | Inherited UT Registration Plus course-evaluation shortcut. |

## Remote code
No. All JavaScript is in the package. Optional dataset updates download data files (SQLite/JSON) only, verified by SHA-256.

## Data usage disclosures (Privacy practices tab)
- Collects: **none** transmitted. Handles locally: "Personally identifiable information" (only if present in an imported audit), "Educational information / academic records" (imported audit, schedules) — stored locally only.
- Certify: not sold; not used for unrelated purposes; not used for creditworthiness.
- Privacy policy URL: publish `docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md` (e.g. GitHub Pages) and link it.
