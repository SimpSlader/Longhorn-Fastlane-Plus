Fastlane Course Planner — offline demo

Not a UT page. These are local fixtures shaped like UT’s course schedule and add/drop screens. Nothing here contacts UT.

1. Set up a demo schedule

Creates a schedule named “Fastlane demo (Ideal)” in your extension (UGS 302 MWF 9, M 408C MWF 10 + TTH 11, MUS 302L live online MW 1) with two ranked backups. Remove it any time.

2. Course listing with inline grades, async highlighting and conflicts

Grade percentages come from the bundled historical dataset (real counts). Change the threshold in the “Plus” column header. Blue rows are verified asynchronous; “LIVE ONLINE” sections meet at scheduled times. Use “+ Backup” to add ranked backups.

3. Registration screen simulation

The floating assistant (top right) shows the next code from your queue. Copy it (or turn on autofill in Fastlane → Registration prep), then press the simulated Submit yourself. Use the buttons to simulate UT responses and mark outcomes in the assistant. Your real queue is used: Undo or “Reset progress” afterward.
